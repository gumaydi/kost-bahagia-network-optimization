## Optional post-project validation protocol

Record a sanitized per-AP inventory, verify cable/link status, collect repeatable signal and throughput readings for each floor, and perform walking-path tests during continuous ping or calls. Capture reassociation time and any packet loss to distinguish perceived seamless usage from measured roaming performance. Retain management passwords, resident information, serial numbers and complete MAC inventories outside public materials.
