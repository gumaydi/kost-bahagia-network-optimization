## Historic equipment changes

The **6 December 2025** planning sheet contains a `Hikvision` label for one floor-3 position and `TP-Link AX10` for three others. The lead states that initial equipment was removed and replaced by AX10 devices in the final deployment. Historical device labels must not be read as the installed bill of materials.

## Field tools and materials

Archived preparation records mention RJ45 crimping tools, cutter/stripper, LAN tester, drill and bits, fixings, ladder, screwdriver, pliers, and cutting pliers. Cat5e minimum was discussed; a Cat6 carton appears in photographed equipment. The installed cable class, exact run lengths and certification test results cannot be inferred from a carton.

## Remaining inventory information

The switch make/model, exact switch count beyond the described distribution, ISP modem identifier, port map and cable specification are candidates for an approved sanitized as-built inventory. Passwords, serial numbers and management access details must not be published.
