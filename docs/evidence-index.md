## Confidence and interpretation

- **Recorded** means present in archived messages, images or screenshots, with original timestamps and source references.
- **Field-confirmed** describes firsthand recollection from the project lead, obtained after the 2025 installation, not a device-exported record.
- **Planned** means a design worksheet or proposal, not necessarily the final running configuration.

The old channel worksheet includes a Hikvision device label. It is retained only as design history because the project lead confirms the earlier wireless units were replaced with Archer AX10 APs. The archived 2025 material includes one speed screenshot, but no controlled pre/post throughput study or timing trace for roaming. Missing switch identifiers and exact port assignments are not invented.

## Private source handling

Archived chats, unredacted field photographs, contacts, payment information, **internal network addressing**, and secrets remain outside the public package. The project lead approved disclosure of the public property name, postal address and Google Maps link, as well as sanitized field photographs, on 6 November 2026. This does not authorize disclosure of private occupant details or management credentials.
